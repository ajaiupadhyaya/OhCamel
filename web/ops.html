what this page cannot see

Nothing persists, so there is no uptime percentage and no record of the last smoke run; its two load-bearing assertions — the counter advancing across two seconds and frames spread across a window rather than piled at its end — are re-run in this browser instead, in the strips above.

CPU share, Caddy's memory, request counts per route and the certificate's expiry are not engine-observable; docker stats and Caddy's stdout have them. hostname is the container id, not the droplet.

From the public origin the live book is not visible, by name or by number: it is gated, and this page does not route around the password. The peer column is filled only from the live origin, over the demo engine's own CORS header, and nothing crosses the other way.

The stream state is this browser's reading. A proxy that dropped the stream silently would read as parked until the browser reconnected; what separates parked from dead is that /api/ops still answers, and that is the reading used.

The counters under process are Incremental's for the whole process — the startup probe and every fork /api/stress makes — and are not this graph's size. book moving is the one rate a fork cannot inflate.

this is the one page that asks on a timer, because a process's uptime is not a graph change polls /api/ops every 10 s while this tab is visible · sixty polls is ten minutes
