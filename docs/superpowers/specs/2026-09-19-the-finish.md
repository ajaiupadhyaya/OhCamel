# OhCamel: the finish

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Status:** binding. It replaces the A4 plan and the separate A5 and A6 phase plans.
**Governing spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-12-the-desk-design.md` remains the design of record. This document says what finishing it means, what is superseded, which rulings carry forward, what is deliberately left out, and how completion is proved.
**Implementation plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-19-final-completion.md`.
**Revision:** second. Revised against a pre-dispatch review that checked every file the plan named against the repository. The corrections it produced are recorded in §3.7, because each one is a decision and not a typo.

## 1. Scope: what 100% means

The owner asked for every function and operation finished, deploy-ready and documented. That is five things, and nothing less counts.

**1. Every clause of the design spec is built, or recorded as a departure with its reason.** §3.13 (risk depth), §3.14 (self-validation) and §3.15 (operations) go from roughly 15%, 0% and 10% built to complete. Every clause of the already-merged phases that the survey found half-built is finished too: the journal's `alerts` table gets a writer, a hand halt survives a restart, `/api/ops` states the desk's trading state in words as §8 item 5 requires, TCA aggregates per order as §3.9 says, the navigation follows §4's order, the desk states its market clock on the wire so a host script can read it without a credential, and the public demo shows every arc §0 promises — research, signal, size, rules, gate, order, venue, fill, validation.

**2. Nothing in the repository claims something that is not true, and a claim that is a count is checked by CI.** Today the site says GARCH is "implemented, not wired in" in twenty-odd places, says options are "DISABLED — no options-chain source", says the live track record "is phase A5", says the research suite has 355 tests when it has 388, and reports line counts a year stale. Every such sentence becomes true or goes. A count that a document quotes is wrapped in a marker and compared with the code **by a CI job**, not by a Makefile target alone — a target no job runs is how 355 survived in three documents, and the plan's own anti-drift promise would otherwise be unenforced.

**3. Main is deployable by one owner command, and rolls back by another.** Both images are built and smoke-tested in CI and published to GHCR; the droplet pulls an immutable sha; the on-droplet fallback builds **both** images, because a fallback that builds one leaves the other stale — which is the exact defect `deploy/deploy.sh` has today. A pre-deploy backup is taken; the book is validated before the engine restarts; the smoke suite checks the desk on both hosts, including that the paper account is reachable and that the mutating routes refuse a request without the header. A nightly journal backup exists, is verified when written, is rotated, and restores.

**4. Every number a test asserts is derived by hand, and every identity holds to 1e-9.** That is §5's A4 bar and §6's standing rule, and it applies to A5's coverage statistics — every one of them, not only Kupiec — to the watchdog's thresholds, to the re-measured per-tick cost, and to A6's retention arithmetic as much as to the factor model's Euler split.

**5. What only the owner can do is written down as exact commands, and nothing is claimed done on the owner's behalf.** The agent cannot ssh to the droplet, cannot read a credential, cannot edit `book.sexp`, and cannot build a Docker image locally. Deploys, book edits, the one live paper fill, the GHCR visibility switch, the restore drill and the key rotation are the owner's. The status file records each only from what the owner reports.

Completion is therefore a property of the repository plus a named list of owner steps — not a property of the droplet. Two acceptance rows are additionally gated by time and cannot be pulled forward: the live VaR record prints no verdict before 60 scored sessions, and the 250-session window closes around September 2027. The plan finishes the code that will print those verdicts; the calendar prints them. Where the calendar *can* be brought forward it is: the long window and the two new estimators ship as their own stage, ahead of the factor model and the pages, so `garch` and `cornish_fisher` begin accruing scored sessions at the earliest deploy the work allows rather than at the end of a long stage.

## 3. Architecture decisions carried forward

### 3.1 The invariants are unchanged

Spec §2's invariants bind every task. Four of them do most of the work here:

- **Invariant 6 — the kernel cannot place an order.** `lib/` may not name the desk. CI greps `lib/` for `paper-api\.alpaca\.markets|/v2/orders|Sqlite3|ohcamel_desk` and the grep must stay silent. A4's new feeds talk only to `data.alpaca.markets` and FRED, and cannot use the desk's `Desk_time`. This finish extends the invariant: **`lib/` also writes no file.** `lib/alerts.ml`'s `File` sink is retired, because a kernel module opening a writer contradicts both invariant 6 and invariant 13, and the journal's `alerts` table now has a real writer to take its place.
- **Invariant 9 — paper only by construction.** The trading host stays the constant `paper-api.alpaca.markets` and a key that does not begin `PK` is refused before any request. No task changes this, and no task places a live order.
- **Invariants 10–12 — journal before wire, fills are facts, every trade passes the engine first.** One submit site behind the `Wire` permit. This finish makes that a compiler fact rather than a test fact by adding `desk/oms.mli`. It is the only interface file this finish adds outside a module it also creates, and it is in `desk/` — see §3.6's ruling on interface files.
- **Invariant 13 — persistence is the journal, and only the journal.** A backup is a copy of the journal, taken with SQLite's online backup API, written to the host at `/var/backups/ohcamel` by a one-shot container that mounts the data volume and reads it. The engine neither writes nor reads backups; their staleness is the host watchdog's business. The root filesystem stays read-only.

### 3.2 The seams stay seams

`lib/server.ml` learns nothing about the desk, the options feed or validation. Everything the desk contributes to a frame joins through `?frame_extra`; everything the desk contributes to `/api/ops` joins through a new, equally generic `?ops_extra`, which refuses a key that collides with a core key. The kernel's own long-window status goes straight into `json_of_ops`, because the long window is a kernel feed.

### 3.3 Fits are cells, never nodes

A4's central decision survives intact and is the reason the phase is safe: every regression, factor covariance and GARCH fit runs **outside stabilization**, in `Graph.set_long_panel`, and writes its result into a cell. `Graph.fork` recreates nodes, so a fit expressed as a node would refit on every fork — and a six-leg rebalance is gated as up to 63 subsets. A `Graph.For_testing.fits_run` counter proves the rule: it stays at 0 across a tick, a fill, a fork and a six-leg `Gate.check`.

### 3.4 Unknown is `None`, and stays `None`

A figure the engine cannot compute renders as a reason, never as 0, and a limit on such a figure is reported unevaluable. This covers too few observations, a singular regression, missing volume, a contract not yet marked, a name bought after the GARCH weights were frozen, a session whose cash flows could not be fetched, a validation window with fewer than 60 scored pairs, and a bar store not yet warm enough to compute a signal's data hash.

### 3.5 A4's rulings 1–12, carried forward

All twelve bind the risk-depth work unchanged, with the two amendments their reviews produced. In summary, with the amendments marked:

1. **The long window** holds 250 daily returns per name beside the 60-observation window: consolidated (SIP) daily bars with `adjustment=all`, only final bars (a bar dated *d* is used once `now` is at or after 00:16 UTC on *d*+1), refreshed at startup and at 00:20, 06:20, 12:20 and 18:20 UTC, and rebuilt idempotently rather than pushed at the roll.
2. **The calendar is the factor ETFs'.** Panel dates are the dates on which SPY, IWM, IWD, IWF and MTUM all have a final bar; each instrument aligns to that calendar on its own and nothing is carried over a gap; ADV is each name's own last 20 final bars; ΔDGS10 uses the last level FRED published on or before each date, and is `nan` on the trailing run of dates FRED has not reached.
3. **The five factors** in fixed order: market = r(SPY); size = r(IWM) − r(SPY); value = r(IWD) − r(IWF); momentum = r(MTUM) − r(SPY); rates = ΔDGS10 in percentage points. The ETFs are fetched over REST and never streamed, so they cost nothing against the 30-symbol stream cap.
4. **Units.** An exposure is dollars per 1.00 of the factor (per 1 pp for rates); exposures use the graph's dollar exposures; variances are dollars squared; the model's VaR is a sibling figure and never the book's VaR.
5. **The regression** is OLS with intercept by Owl's QR, one per instrument, on that name's non-`nan` rows; refused with a reason below 120 observations or when the standardised design's σ_min ÷ σ_max falls below **0.01** — *amended after Task 3's review, which found that 1e-8 accepted betas of ±9,000*. Factor covariance uses the population divisor; residual variance divides by Tᵢ − K − 1; the model assumes uncorrelated residuals and the page says so beside xᵀΣx.
6. **Liquidity.** Participation is its own book setting (`(liquidity ((participation 0.10) (impact_coefficient 1.0)))`), distinct from the order rule's `max_adv_participation`. Impact follows Tóth et al. (2011). LVaR follows Bangia et al. (1999) with the spread-volatility term at 0, because no spread history exists: LVaR = VaR + Σ |x_S| × half_spread_S, with the half-spreads from the desk block.
7. **GARCH(1,1) and Cornish–Fisher on the long window,** both on the listwise common rows of the frozen-weight names, needing at least 240 of them; fits as cells; the frozen weights re-taken after the desk's first applied sync; Cornish–Fisher `None` when the expansion is not strictly increasing on z ∈ [−4, 4], decided **in closed form** — *amended after Task 7a's review, because a sampled grid can miss a violation narrower than its spacing*.
8. **Options: quantities from the book, marks from the indicative feed.** Contracts are declared in the book; an expired one is excluded with a startup warning; implied vol comes from `feed=indicative`, polled every five minutes in chunks of at most 100; the engine computes its own Greeks and Alpaca's are a cross-check column; every cell says *indicative*; an unmarked contract makes a Greek limit in its scope unevaluable.
9. **The demo** seeds a synthetic long panel from its own RNG with known betas, labels it synthetic, declares no options, and gains A4's nodes in Figure 1 as a deliberate topology change.
10. **The desk's order rule keeps its IEX ADV.** A4 does not change it; the docs name the SIP-versus-IEX difference as a limit beside the liquidity view.
11. **The roll's forecasts for A5** are written from a fork at the journal's marks for the date, so a late panel writes exactly the rows an on-time one would, and A5 never scores a forecast that has seen its own outcome.
12. **Exact forms:** book syntax follows `(kind (Factor_exposure (market 2000000.0)))` and `(kind (Greek_limit (gamma 400.0)))`; population divisors throughout except the regression's residual variance; `Graph.create` validates the liquidity pair because node bodies may not raise; every fetch passes Cohttp's `~interrupt` alongside `Clock_ns.with_timeout`; option Greeks are per-share Black–Scholes, as Alpaca's are.

### 3.6 New decisions this finish makes

Stated here as decisions; the plan states them as binding rulings with their tests.

- **Stages ship, and there are eight of them.** Every stage ends merged to `main`, green in CI, with both images published for the merge sha. No work waits unmerged across a stage boundary, and `main` is always one owner command from deployed. Risk depth is **two** stages rather than one: the long window and the two new estimators first, so their forecast rows begin accruing at the earliest deploy; then the factor model, liquidity, the pages and the re-measured figures. Splitting it moves the first `garch` and `cornish_fisher` verdict forward by roughly the length of the second half.
- **Journal changes are additive.** `CREATE TABLE`/`CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS`, no `schema_version` bump. The journal refuses a version it does not know, so a bump would make rollback across it impossible without restoring a backup and losing the orders since. Any task that believes it needs a bump stops and is ruled on. The consequence is concrete for the `alerts` table, whose `limit_name` and `line` columns are `NOT NULL`: a hand halt has no limit, so halt and reset rows carry the literal `kill_switch` in `limit_name` and the reason in `line`. That is a convention, not a schema change.
- **`lib/` has no interface files, and this finish adds none.** There is no `.mli` anywhere under `lib/` today. Authoring a complete `Graph` interface would be a large, unscoped change inside the most serial lane of the plan, and it buys nothing this finish needs. Every kernel task edits the `.ml` alone. The one new interface outside a new module is `desk/oms.mli`, which exists to turn "one submit site" from a test into a compile error. New `desk/` modules ship with their own small interfaces, as `desk/journal.mli` and the other four already do.
- **The live record's loss, derived rather than asserted.** Equity moves by P&L plus net external cash flow: E_t = E_{t−1} + PnL_t + F_t. So **loss_t = E_{t−1} − E_t + F_t**, with a deposit positive. An exceedance is loss_t > VaR_notional_{t−1} for that estimator. This corrects a sign that one draft of this work had backwards.
- **The scorer reuses the statistics and never the harness.** `Var_backtest.Estimator.t` is a closed three-variant type whose `estimate` feeds `rolling`, which produces the `make backtest` and `make backtest-crisis` tables this finish must leave byte-identical. So the live scorer must not extend it and must not call `Var_backtest.run`, which requires one. It calls the statistic functions directly, keyed by the estimator's *name* as the journal records it. The one joint statistic that exists only inside `run` — conditional coverage, Kupiec plus independence at two degrees of freedom — is lifted into a named exported function that `run` then calls, so there is one implementation and not two.
- **Every published forecast is scored.** A forecast made on windows that did not roll is counted and flagged, never dropped after its outcome is known. Dropping an observation because of something learned afterwards is a post-hoc filter and would flatter the record.
- **The zone is named honestly.** The engine's confidence is 0.95, so `Var_backtest.traffic_light` is a binomial traffic light at 95%, not Basel's 99% zone. The page says which, and §8 records it.
- **The demo's horizon is scaled where the forecast is written, not where it is scored.** A demo session spans 20 synthetic daily bars, so its journaled session forecast is scaled by √20 and the page says so. The scorer then needs no demo special case. The task verifies that the demo's per-bar draws are i.i.d. with zero mean before relying on √20, and stops and reports if they are not.
- **The demo may show a synthetic arc, but may never be mistaken for a live one.** The demo's strategy carries a `synthetic:` validation manifest, which R6 accepts only when the venue is simulated; the live intake refuses it and names R6, pinned by a test. Every synthetic date, session and figure is labelled on the page.
- **The desk states its market clock on the wire.** `Venue.Session_clock` already carries `is_open`, `next_open`, `next_close` and `next_close_date`, and the desk already reads it. Nothing publishes it, so the host watchdog's two most valuable checks — a stale sync while the market is open, and no session row thirty minutes after the close — are unimplementable without a credential. The desk therefore caches its newest reading and `/api/desk` and the `/api/ops` desk block carry it, with the stamp of when it was read and a source field, before any script that needs it is written.
- **R8 is decided by evidence, and enforced only after it has been observed.** A code read can establish that two fetch paths agree on feed, adjustment and bar finality; it cannot establish that their closes are identical, because the agent holds no credential and fetches no data. So the gate is equivalence of source, adjustment and timing, and the enforcement is staged: R8 ships in `observe` mode, where a mismatch defers the signal with both hashes recorded and never refuses it, and the owner promotes it to `enforce` from the book once the record shows no mismatch. A hash the core cannot yet compute always defers. If the two paths are not equivalent, R8 is a permanent §8 departure stating the exact difference and no recipe is built.
- **Buy-and-hold is post-hoc and descriptive.** EXP-A01's pre-registered battery, manifests and verdicts are byte-identical; the benchmark lives in its own file, labelled not-a-gate, and the owner may veto it.
- **TCA's primary unit is the order,** with the quantity-weighted fill price and costs recomputed per order, and count, mean, median and quantity-weighted mean for every metric, overall and by symbol. Per-fill rows stay as detail. A2 shipped per-fill only; §3.9 asks for per order.
- **The nav follows §4's order:** Desk, Risk, Research, Execution, Argument, Ops.
- **A count is a CI fact.** The OCaml suite already asserts its own count against `lib/verified.ml`. The three prose numbers, and the Python count, are wrapped in markers and compared by a `lint` job that runs on every push, so a document can no longer disagree with the code.
- **Baseline artefacts and coverage come from a dedicated local clone.** `make` assumes the primary checkout: coverage instrumentation, `_coverage/`, the six-mode gate's paths and `uv` in `research/`. A `make` run from a git worktree can clobber the primary checkout's artefacts, which is why the worktree rule forbids it — but four acceptance criteria are `make`-diff-based and coverage must be measured per stage. The resolution is a full local clone under `$TMPDIR`, which has its own `_coverage/` and its own outputs and shares nothing but the object store. The Stage 0 baseline is produced there once, from `main` at `b6132c6`, and every later diff is against those saved files. Coverage is measured there too, at each stage branch's tip, and the figures are transcribed onto the branch. The primary checkout is never switched for either, so the owner's untracked files there are never at risk.

## 4. Out of scope, and why

These are not gaps. They are the shape of the thing, and they stay stated in `README.md` and `docs/status.md` rather than fixed.

**Inherent to the account and the data.** Paper trading only, by construction. Marks are IEX last prints and quotes are the IEX BBO, not the consolidated tape or the NBBO. The free plan caps the stream at 30 symbols, which is why the factor ETFs are fetched over REST. Paper fills are simulated, so the TCA numbers measure Alpaca's simulator and not a market. Alpaca's history begins in 2016. Signals trade ETFs only, because the data has no delisted names. Option marks are indicative. The liquidity view uses SIP volume while the order rule uses IEX volume, and the two differ by an order of magnitude.

**Inherent to the models.** European options only, one flat rate, one vol per contract, no dividends, no strike buckets, and no implied-vol solve. The factor model assumes uncorrelated residuals. Bangia's spread-volatility term is held at 0, because there is no spread history. GARCH treats the listwise common rows as consecutive. LVaR excludes impact cost and horizons beyond one day, which are shown separately.

**Inherent to statistics.** The live VaR record has no statistical power for months. No verdict prints below 60 scored sessions, and the window is 250. Both EXP-A01 strategies fail their gates, so nothing is promotable; that is a result, not a defect, and a new pre-registered experiment is the owner's hypothesis to write, not a function to finish.

**Inherent to one droplet.** One host, no replica, no staging environment. The equity trail is in memory and rebuilt from the journal at startup. RSS reads "not on this platform" on macOS. Decision Q10 forbids a spending change, so paid DigitalOcean backups and Spaces are the owner's call and the off-box copy defaults to a free pull to the laptop.

**Deliberately not attempted.** A holiday-aware market-on-open window *is* fixed, because A5's session pairing needs a calendar anyway — but a second market-data vendor, a live trading path, an options trading path, a second droplet, a staging host, a migration to a smaller droplet (a disk cannot shrink, so it is a rebuild), and any promotion to `(sizing live)` are all out of scope. `claudecodehandoff.md` describes the superseded Alpha plan, is untracked, and is the owner's file.

**Two shortfalls that are recorded rather than closed.** The live-host smoke suite deliberately never POSTs `/api/desk/kill`, because a regression there would halt the live desk; §3.15's acceptance asks that the mutating routes refuse a headerless request on the live host, and that route is covered only by the hermetic tests. And the research service's exchange calendar is a cache with a weekday fallback: when Alpaca's calendar cannot be fetched it runs on weekdays as it does today, and says so in its log. Both are §8 items, not silent gaps.

## 5. Definition of done

Done is the conjunction of all seventeen. Each is checkable by a command or by a named test.

1. **One history.** `main` contains every stage. `git branch` shows only `main`; `git worktree list` shows only the primary checkout. The tags `archive/a4-plan-v2` and `archive/agent-a3-leftover` resolve. Every earlier plan carries its superseded-or-historical header and appears in the plans index.
2. **Green everywhere.** On `main`: `build-and-test` (ubuntu and macos), `lint`, `coverage`, `research-test`, `image` and `uptime` are all green, and the image workflow has published `ghcr.io/ajaiupadhyaya/ohcamel:<final sha>` and `ghcr.io/ajaiupadhyaya/ohcamel-research:<final sha>`.
3. **Counts cannot drift, and CI is what stops them.** `dune runtest`'s two counts equal `lib/verified.ml`'s `tests` and `scheduler_tests`; `pytest`'s collected count equals `research/src/ohcamel_research/verified.py`'s `TESTS`; the `lint` job runs `make check-counts` on every push, and editing any quoted count in `README.md`, `docs/overview.md` or `docs/status.md` without the code makes that job fail.
4. **The published tables did not move.** `make backtest`, `make backtest-crisis`, `make stress` and `make options`, and `make garch`'s table rows, are byte-identical to the Stage 0 baseline captured from `main` at `b6132c6` in a dedicated local clone. The single permitted difference is the sentence `make garch` prints about wiring.
5. **The invariants hold.** `grep -rE 'paper-api\.alpaca\.markets|/v2/orders|Sqlite3|ohcamel_desk' lib/` is silent, and so is a grep for `Writer.with_file` under `lib/`. `desk/oms.mli` makes it a compile error for anything outside `desk/oms.ml` to reach the venue's submit. No `.mli` exists under `lib/`.
6. **Nothing false is left on the page or in the docs.** A grep over `web/`, `bin/`, `lib/`, `README.md`, `docs/` and `Makefile` finds no "not wired" GARCH claim, no options "DISABLED", no "no options-chain source", no "phase A5", and no "nothing on this page makes the comparison yet". R8's sentence says which of its three states it is in: observing, enforcing, or recorded in §8 as a departure.
7. **A4's identities hold to 1e-9,** each by a named test: OLS residuals orthogonal to every regressor; Euler contributions summing to the systematic variance; systematic plus idiosyncratic equal to the model total; LVaR equal to VaR at zero spread; Cornish–Fisher equal to the normal quantile at g₁ = g₂ = 0; liquidity totals equal to the sums of their lines; the graph's GARCH and Cornish–Fisher figures equal to the pure functions on the same rows; and `Var_backtest.conditional_coverage` equal to `run`'s field on the same exceedance series.
